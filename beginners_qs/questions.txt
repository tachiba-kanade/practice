1. Coin Flip Simulator
Simulate 10,000 coin flips and plot the distribution of outcomes.
Then extend it, what is the probability of getting more than 5,200 heads in 10,000 flips?
Solve it analytically using the central limit theorem then verify with your simulation.
One project. Probability, simulation, and the relationship between theory and computation, three things quant firms test directly.

2. Dice Probability Calculator
Build a function that calculates the probability of any outcome when rolling any number of fair dice.
Extend it to biased dice.
Then solve a classic interview problem, what is the expected number ot rolls to see all six faces of a fair die?
A direct interview preparation tool dressed up as a coding exercise.

3. Simple Moving Average Strategy
Download price data for one asset using yfinance.
Calculate a short and long moving average.
Go long when the short crosses above the long. Exit when it crosses
Calculate the Sharpe ratio.
The strategy does not need to be profitable. Understanding what you are measuring is the point.

4. Monte Carlo Retirement Calculator
Simulate 10,000 portfolio paths over 30 years using random annual returns.
Plot the distribution of final values.
Calculate the probability of running out of money before year 30.
Quant thinking applied to a problem everyone understands, uniquely easy to explain in any interview.

5. Correlation Matrix Visualiser
Download price data for 10 stocks across different sectors.
Calculate the correlation matrix of daily returns and plot it as a heatmap.
Then ask, which stocks are most correlated? What does that mean for a portfolio holding all of them?
Two hours to build. Teaches the foundation of portfolio risk management.
